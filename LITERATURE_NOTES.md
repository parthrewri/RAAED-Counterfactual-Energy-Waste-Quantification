# Initial literature notes (verify and expand before submission)

1. UCI Individual Household Electric Power Consumption dataset. 2,075,259 one-minute observations from one household in Sceaux, France, Dec 2006-Nov 2010; includes three sub-metering channels and missing readings. DOI: 10.24432/C58K54. Dataset page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/235/individual%2Bhousehold%2Belectric%2Bpower%2Bconsumption

2. "Anomaly detection and prediction of energy consumption for smart homes using machine learning", ETRI Journal, first published 30 September 2024, DOI: 10.4218/etrij.2023-0155. This work combines outlier detection and predictive models for smart-home energy data. It demonstrates why a simple forecast-plus-anomaly combination is not itself a sufficient novelty claim.

3. Mascali et al., "A machine learning-based Anomaly Detection Framework for building electricity consumption data", Sustainable Energy, Grids and Networks, Dec 2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.segan.2023.101194. The paper discusses unsupervised anomaly detection, interpretability, and synthetic ground truth for building electricity consumption.

These are starting points, not a systematic review. Add papers on counterfactual explanations, energy baselining, measurement and verification, residual-based anomaly detection, and energy disaggregation. Distinguish the proposed workflow from each paper.
